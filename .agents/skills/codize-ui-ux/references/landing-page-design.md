# Landing Page Design

## Lead with the user's pain and product mechanism

- **Principle:** Tell a coherent story from the 80% Trap to the Build Loop to Project Defense Report.
- **Why:** A visually impressive page is useless if the user cannot tell what Codize does or why it matters.
- **Use:** Hero, problem, mechanism, payoff, and closing CTA.
- **Do not use:** Do not shame AI use, present the 80% Trap as a measured statistic, or imply proven outcomes without pilot evidence.
- **Anti-pattern:** Generic productivity claims, feature-card soup, or a dramatic visual unrelated to the workflow.
- **Codize:** Keep “AI built your first 80%” as named-pattern framing; explain that Codize trains plan, prompt, review, verify, explain, and reflect; show the external AI tool's role honestly.
- **Source:** `[A1, A2; S5 00:00–01:25]`

## Preserve the identity while allowing narrative space

- **Principle:** Use the established dark/violet developer-tool system with larger composition and fewer, stronger scenes.
- **Why:** The landing page can be expressive without suggesting a different product.
- **Use:** Full-bleed scenes, technical panels, workflow demos, and report preview.
- **Do not use:** Do not reintroduce a contrasting editorial typeface, generic SaaS dashboard grids, or decoration that competes with the message.
- **Anti-pattern:** Every section is a glass card; every sentence has an animation; giant empty gutters on wide screens.
- **Codize:** Keep DM Sans-led landing typography, shared gutter rules, centered scenes, readable measures, restrained glass on signature demos, and actual product labels.
- **Source:** `[A2, A6; S4 00:35–04:22; S6 01:15–04:28]`

## Demonstrate the workflow, do not fake the product

- **Principle:** Simulations must teach relationships and be clearly labeled.
- **Why:** Product demonstrations create understanding only when their status and limits are honest.
- **Use:** Patch-loop terminal, Build Loop, simulated Defense, and report preview.
- **Do not use:** Do not run fake AI calls, invent hidden evaluator reasoning, show scores, or imply a simulation is live evidence.
- **Anti-pattern:** A beautiful fake IDE that makes Codize look like a coding agent.
- **Codize:** Label the Defense scene “simulated preview,” keep Generate attributed to “your AI tool,” and show the report as the payoff of the student's workflow record.
- **Source:** `[A1, A2, A6]`

## Use motion for narrative continuity

- **Principle:** Motion should reveal cause, progression, or transfer of control.
- **Why:** A landing story can benefit from staged continuity, but scroll effects can also steal control.
- **Use:** Patch-loop progression, Build Loop stage focus, and Defense exchange when stable and accessible.
- **Do not use:** Avoid scroll-jacking, content that exists only after motion, resize feedback loops, and decorative perpetual animation.
- **Anti-pattern:** Motion added to every element or a sticky panel that changes height and self-scrolls.
- **Codize:** Keep fixed-size sticky content areas, `overflow-anchor: none` where needed, hysteresis around scroll stage changes, complete reduced-motion/no-JS states, and tap/keyboard alternatives.
- **Source:** `[S5 03:32–04:28; S6 13:50–15:31; A2, A6]`

## Keep one CTA hierarchy

- **Principle:** Make the primary action unmistakable and the secondary action genuinely secondary.
- **Why:** Competing calls to action increase decision cost.
- **Use:** Hero and closing scene.
- **Do not use:** Do not repeat many differently worded primary actions across every scene.
- **Anti-pattern:** Several accent buttons with equal weight.
- **Codize:** Preserve “Stop Debugging Blindly” as the primary CTA when it fits current direction; use a quieter workflow/report exploration action as secondary.
- **Source:** `[S1 00:24–01:05; S7 31:00–32:27; A1, A6]`

## Landing verification

Check:

1. The product is understandable without interacting with animation.
2. The 80% claim remains qualified and citations remain compact and readable.
3. Every simulation is labeled and contains no scores/private reasoning.
4. Scenes work at 390px, tablet, 1080p, and 1920px without horizontal overflow.
5. Keyboard, touch, no-JS, and reduced-motion paths expose the same core content.
6. Motion does not resize sticky panels or trap scrolling.
7. The landing still looks and sounds like the protected Codize product.
